How we work

Four steps, one point of contact, no surprises on the day.

  1. 1

    Consultation

    We visit your venue, hear your ideas and agree on a budget.

  2. 2

    Design

    You approve a mood board, colour palette and stage layout before we build anything.

  3. 3

    Setup

    Our crew installs stages, flowers and lighting the day before, so nothing is rushed.

  4. 4

    Event day

    A coordinator stays on site, and we clear everything away when the night ends.

Questions we hear often

How early should I book?

For weddings, six to eight weeks ahead is comfortable. Smaller events can often be arranged in two weeks.

Can you work at any venue?

Yes. We visit first, then plan around the venue's size, power supply and access times.

Can I change the design after booking?

Yes, you can change details until ten days before the event.

What does setup and clear-out cover?

Delivery, installation, an on-site team during the event, and full removal afterwards.

How do payments work?

A booking advance confirms your date. The balance is due before setup begins.
